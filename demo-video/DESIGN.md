# HackStop demo visual direction

Use the portal's actual light interface as the visual source. Keep the canvas warm white, frame screenshots with thin gray borders, and use the existing blue for emphasis. Typography is Inter, with strong black headings and quiet gray support text. Motion is a restrained horizontal slide and fade between complete screens. Avoid gradients, glowing effects, icons, stock imagery, and decorative interface elements.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#FAFAFA`
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Ink: `#0A0A0A`
- Secondary text: `#525252`
- Border: `#E5E7EB`
- Action: `#2563EB`

## Typography

- Inter, 700 for headings and 400 for captions.

## What not to do

- Do not add features absent from the captured portal.
- Do not use screenshots as blurred or color-treated backdrops.
- Do not invent scores, rankings, or testimonials.
